{#- The brief carried through a form as hidden fields. Why hidden fields rather than a hidden JSON blob or a server-side draft row: the values that come back are re-validated through the same :class:`ResearchBrief` schema as the generated ones, so a user who edits the brief gets exactly the same caps and cleaning. A JSON blob would either be trusted blindly or have to be parsed twice. Lists are newline-separated because that is what the edit form posts, and ``_list_field`` in the schema accepts either form. -#}
